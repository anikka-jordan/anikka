About

Hi, I'm Ani, and I'm from Michigan. I like drawing, writing poems, and taking pretty pictures of brains. Most of my work, both creative and academic, revolves around human memory: how it makes us who we are, how our environment shapes it, and the biology behind how well it functions, especially in educational contexts.

I am currently a Ph.D. student in the Interdepartmental Neuroscience Program at Yale University. Previously, I got my B.S. in Psychology and English from Emory University and worked in Chicago for a few years.

Thanks for dropping by! Hope you enjoy.
